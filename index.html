We put Sciences Po students to work on real problems for *real *clients: companies, public institutions and associations that need sharp research and *clear *recommendations.

Our consultants study politics, economics, law and history on one of the most international campuses in France. That mix shows up in the work: careful analysis, several languages, and a feel for how institutions actually run.

Every mission is staffed, supervised and quality-checked by the association, and delivered on a fixed scope and timeline you agree to up front.
